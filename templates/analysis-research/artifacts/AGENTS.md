# AGENTS.md

Scope: `templates/analysis-research/artifacts/`

## Purpose

- This subtree stores derived outputs, summaries, audits, and handoff-ready artifacts.

## Rules

- Every artifact should be able to point back to supporting notes or sources.
- Artifacts may sharpen presentation, but they may not invent new unsupported major claims.
- If the output is still exploratory, keep it in `notes/` instead of dressing it up here.

## Stop conditions

- A polished artifact with no visible support chain is rejected.
- If the artifact sounds more certain than its evidence allows, downgrade or re-review before exit.
