# Sources

Use `sources/` for imported or copied evidence with provenance intact.

## Belongs here

- raw source documents, excerpts, transcripts, links, and captured reference material
- copied evidence that should remain close to its original wording or structure
- source-side metadata needed to trace origin later

## Does not belong here

- your interpretation or comparative reasoning
- polished conclusions or recommendations
- hidden edits that make the source read cleaner than it really was

## Inspect first

Before writing here, confirm the file is evidence intake rather than analysis. If you are explaining or comparing it, that belongs in `notes/` or `artifacts/`.
