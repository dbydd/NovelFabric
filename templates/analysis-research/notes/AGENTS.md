# AGENTS.md

Scope: `templates/analysis-research/notes/`

## Purpose

- This subtree stores working interpretation, structured comparison, and maintained reasoning.

## Rules

- Every substantive claim should preserve named support paths.
- Distinguish observation, interpretation, and open question instead of smoothing them together.
- If evidence is partial, downgrade claim strength rather than polishing certainty.

## Stop conditions

- Unsupported certainty fails the gate.
- A note that changes stance without reopening support files must be rolled back or re-reviewed.
