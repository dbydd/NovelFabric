# AGENTS.md

Scope: `templates/novel-basic/artifacts/`

## Purpose

- This subtree stores derived narrative products: audits, comparison notes, promotion plans, and frozen outputs.

## Rules

- Artifacts must say what was inspected, what was inferred, and what remains unresolved.
- Artifacts may support canon work, but they may not pretend to be canon.
- If canon changes make an artifact misleading, mark it stale or freeze it before more truth ships.

## Stop conditions

- Artifact text that sounds authoritative without promotion links is rejected.
- A stale artifact left unlabeled fails the gate.
