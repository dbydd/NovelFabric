# AGENTS.md

Scope: `templates/novel-basic/inbox/`

## Purpose

- This subtree stores intake, proposals, rough notes, and unresolved material.

## Rules

- Preserve source wording, uncertainty, and status where they matter.
- Treat inbox items as candidates, not truth.
- If an item is promoted, keep the promotion trail visible; do not erase intake history.

## Stop conditions

- Polishing intake into canon-like prose without promotion is rejected.
- Deleting ambiguity markers to make a note look cleaner fails review.
