# `novel-basic`

Narrative workspace harness for long-form fiction and worldbuilding.

## Use this template when

- accepted story truth needs a stable home in `canon/`
- raw intake, editorial proposals, and contradiction reports need to stay separate in `inbox/`
- derived audits, packs, and working outputs belong in `artifacts/`
- continuity control matters more than prose smoothness

## Do not start here when

- the workspace is still fully generic and should stay root-first
- the main durable surface is reusable tooling rather than narrative truth
- the main job is evidence analysis rather than story continuity

## Layout contract

- `canon/` is accepted truth
- `inbox/` is raw intake and unresolved material
- `artifacts/` is derived output and working product
- these layers are intentionally separate and should not blur

## First read order

1. `AGENTS.md`
2. `SOUL.md`
3. `template.json`
4. `.agents/skills/README.md`
5. the relevant `canon/`, `inbox/`, and `artifacts/` readme files
6. the specific files that govern the current story subject

## Typical first moves

- inspect nearby canon before editing any truth-bearing file
- keep unresolved ideas in `inbox/` until promotion is explicit
- stage comparisons, audits, and packs in `artifacts/`
- split style cleanup from canon changes when they would otherwise mix intent

## Protected and audited surfaces

Protected paths are declared in `template.json`.
In this template, the root harness files, `.agents/skills/**`, and `canon/**` are the highest-pressure surfaces and should change through explicit promotion or continuity reasoning.

## Success condition

This template is working when canon remains authoritative, intake stays recoverable, artifacts stay derivative, and continuity decisions remain traceable.
