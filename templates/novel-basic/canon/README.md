# canon/

This directory is the accepted story truth for `WORKSPACE_NAME`.

## What belongs here

- Approved world rules, stable character facts, accepted chronology, canonical places, factions, and resolved scene outcomes.
- Text that downstream writing and audits may treat as authoritative.

## What does not belong here

- Brainstorms, scraps, raw imports, unresolved alternatives, or convenience summaries with no authority trail.
- Derived audits that explain canon without being canon.

## Inspect first

- Open the file that directly governs your subject.
- Then open the nearest neighboring canon file that could break continuity if you change the first one.
- Only after that should you consult `inbox/` proposals or `artifacts/` summaries.

## Rule

If a fact is not yet accepted, keep it out of `canon/`.
