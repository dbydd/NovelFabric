# PROMPT_ENGINEERING_CORE

## Trigger

Use this file when the task may alter canon status, consume inbox material, or produce derived narrative artifacts.

## Dual-channel experiment

- `candidate-a` — canon-safe branch: narrowest truthful move
- `candidate-b` — higher-pressure editorial branch: stronger promotion or correction proposal

Only one branch may survive into the final write.

## Required sequence

1. Name subject, timeframe, and target directories.
2. Inspect canon first, inbox second, derived support third.
3. Draft `candidate-a` and `candidate-b`.
4. Reject the branch that outruns evidence, promotion authority, or continuity safety.
5. Converge to one truth-classification action.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- Do not let a stylish rewrite smuggle canon mutation.
- Do not treat inbox prose as already approved truth.
- Do not let both branches leak into the same canon edit.
- Do not promote without naming source intake or editorial reason.
